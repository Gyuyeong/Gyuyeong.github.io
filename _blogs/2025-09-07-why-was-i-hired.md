While I was working as an intern at SAP Labs Korea, my manager invited me to have lunch together. We entered a brunch cafe, sat down, ordered some food, and had some conversation that wasn’t work related. He asked general questions like “How are you doing?”, “How do you feel working here, is it good? bad?” and etc. I told him I am having a good time learning and experiencing the culture. Then the manager said that he wanted to give me a small gift. However, unlike a usual physical gift, he decided to tell me why he hired me.

**Yes, I’d love to know why you chose me**

It turned out that there were 7 other candidates alongside me that wanted the position at that time. He said they were all competent just like me… at least according to what their resume told him.

**Ok, then what went wrong?**

The reasons he told me were quite shocking. He said that there were roughly 3 categories of bad signs.

## 1. Major Flaw

The interview was conducted online. We turn on our cameras and have some technical and general conversation during the interview. However, 2 of the candidates he said had a major flaw. I don’t know about other countries, but in South Korea, during an online interview, you generally are not allowed to look up on the website for something you don’t know unless you are explicitly told you are allowed. However, during the interview, when they were asked questions, the manager heard a lot of keyboard strokes. Since there was no reason to use the keyboard, it was clear that they were looking up something in the Internet. So, major flaw it is.

## 2. You Are Not Answering My Question Properly

According to him, some candidates just did not answer the question. I didn’t understand what that really meant. He then asked me a question:

**What is the difference between Process and Thread?**

This is a typical question that is frequently asked to a junior applying for the job. If you graduated from computer science department or any other similar ones, this is something you will probably know. I answered the question without too much difficulty.

**A process is a program with separate memory space and resources, while a thread is a smaller unit that shares global and heap memory and only have separate stack area.**

He told me that is a valid answer. However, when he asked the same question to those candidates, this is what they said (not exactly that but you’ll get the idea):

**I spawned process with C using fork and made threads using pthread.**

The question was asking the **difference** between the two units, however, they gave out their experience, which was not what they were asked for. “Many of the conversation were like this”, he said.  They might have been nervous and maybe weren’t able to interpret the question properly, but as a person trying to hire someone, this was a red flag, since he was not able to communicate with them well.

## 3. Are You Trying to Lie to Me?

The last one was a bit different. He recalled that there was one person who could have been chosen since he had better GPA and better project experiences than me. However, maybe the candidate felt like he/she had to look perfect, since his/her resume seemed almost perfect. He/she talked as if he/she knew everything that was asked. However, as a junior, this is simply just not possible. I know after some experience that there are many knowledges and skills that cannot be acquired just by getting good GPA and doing projects. The manager was able to quickly see that this candidate was not being honest. Therefore, he/she was not chosen.

All of these were valuable lessons, but I still have not found out something important

**Ok, then why was I chosen?**

His answers were very clear. He said:

**Because you answered my question, and you said “I don’t know” when you did not know something.**

It turns out admitting that I am not perfect was good enough as a junior. While not knowing everything is not ideal, being honest after hard work did pay off. To this day, I thank my manager for telling me this and will keep this advice for the rest of the journey.
